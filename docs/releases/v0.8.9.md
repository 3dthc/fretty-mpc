# Fretty v0.8.9

## What's new
- **A4 TUNE knob (PERFORM page):** sets the reference pitch for everything Fretty plays, from 415 to 466 Hz (440 by default), so it can match a record or sample that isn't at 440. It's on the 8th Q-Link of the PERFORM page, is saved with your project, and presets never change it.
- **Speaker-grille logo panel:** the chrome Fretty logo now sits on a perforated metal grille, tinted to each theme (green for Surf, violet for Violet), on every page.
- **ECHO SYNC** moved to the foot of the FEEL panel ("ECHO ON THE BEAT") to make room for A4 TUNE; it works as before.

## Fixed
- **Fretboard dots on the wrong note:** with CAPO on, the lit dot sat that many frets too low (it counted from the capo, not from the nut); with DROP D♭ the low string's dots named the wrong notes; a note below the low string showed as the open E; and notes past fret 15 were drawn at fret 15. The dot now always sits on the fret you'd play and names that note, and Fretty prefers a position on the drawn neck when there's a choice.
- **Surf theme:** the logo's grille is now green, so the chrome logo stands out instead of blending into it.
