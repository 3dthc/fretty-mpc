# Fretty v0.8.9

## What's new
- **A4 TUNE knob (PERFORM page):** sets the reference pitch for everything Fretty plays, from 415 to 466 Hz (440 by default), so it can match a record or sample that isn't at 440. It's on the 8th Q-Link of the PERFORM page, is saved with your project, and presets never change it.
- **Speaker-grille logo panel:** the chrome Fretty logo now sits on a perforated metal grille, tinted to each theme, on every page.
- **ECHO SYNC** moved to the foot of the FEEL panel ("ECHO ON THE BEAT") to make room for A4 TUNE; it works as before.
