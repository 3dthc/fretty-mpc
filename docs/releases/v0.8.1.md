**Fretty** is a guitar instrument for MPC standalone. It plays real, clean recordings on a six-string model with an animated fretboard, and adds a synth layer, chord pads and effects. Every sample is CC0, so it's free to share.

![Strumming](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/3dthc/fretty-mpc/c0e47a6/docs/images/strum.gif)

## Instruments
Acoustic archtop (pickup + mic), Electric, Folk Harp, Concert Harp, Strumstick, Violin, Violin Pizz, Dan Tranh, Psaltery, Bowed Psaltery. Velocity picks soft, medium or hard recordings. Each instrument loads the first time you pick it.

## Pages
![PLAY](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/3dthc/fretty-mpc/c0e47a6/docs/images/play_black.png)

**PLAY:** the neck shows each played string and fret.
- **Instrument** ◀ ▶ and **Strum** (Down, Up, Alternate, Off).
- **Sustain / Palm Mute**, **Legato** (hammer-ons and slides on the same string) and **Drop D♭**.
- **Guitar:** Blend (pickup ↔ mic), Tone, Damp, Vibrato, Strum Speed and Noise. Knobs that don't apply to the current instrument are dimmed.
- **FX:** Drive, Chorus, Echo with Echo Time, Space (reverb) and Volume. Each effect is off at zero.
- **Layers:** Pluck (a plucked-string layer) and Mix A / B.

| ![LAYER A](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/3dthc/fretty-mpc/c0e47a6/docs/images/layer_a.png) | ![LAYER B](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/3dthc/fretty-mpc/c0e47a6/docs/images/layer_b.png) |

**LAYER A** (the instrument): Position and Capo; Velocity, Damp and **Glide** (slide into overlapped notes, steel-guitar style); the Pluck layer (level, ring, color, detune); **Swell** (fade notes in) and **Release** (let notes ring on).

**LAYER B** (the synth): Sine, Triangle, Saw or Square, with Octave and Detune; Velocity and Filter Env; an ADSR envelope; Cutoff and Resonance. **A · Guitar** and **B · Synth** turn each layer on or off, and **Mix A / B** balances them. In the middle, both play at full.

![PERFORM](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/3dthc/fretty-mpc/c0e47a6/docs/images/perform.png)

**PERFORM:** **Chord pads.** Pick a key, a scale (Major, Minor, Dorian, Mixolydian, Harmonic Minor or Phrygian), Triads or 7ths, and a voicing (Open, Barre or High). Each pad then strums a full chord, and the tiles light the chord that's playing. **Feel** adds humanized timing and velocity, and **Echo Sync** locks the echo to your project's tempo.

## Presets
50 starters in the PRESET menu, grouped by tag: **ACO** acoustic, **ELE** electric, **80S** chorus-wash and post-punk, **STL** steel and slide, **HRP** harps, **VLN** violin, **ZTH** zithers and dulcimer, **FLK** folk, and **SYN** guitar + synth. Presets never change the theme or the PERFORM settings.

Three themes: MPC Black, Sunburst and MPC Grey.

## Install
Requires a first-generation MPC OS standalone device (32-bit ARM: MPC Live / Live II, One, X, Key 61 or Force) with root SSH access. Installing plugins this way is unofficial, so back up first.

1. Unzip **Fretty-0.8.1-mpc-armv7.zip** and copy the folder to the device: `scp -r Fretty-0.8.1 root@<device-ip>:/tmp/`
2. Run `ssh root@<device-ip> sh /tmp/Fretty-0.8.1/install.sh`. It stops MPC (save first), installs into `/sdcard/Synths`, backs up `MPC.settings` and starts MPC again.
3. Add **Fretty** from the plugin browser (Instrument plugins).

Full instructions are in `INSTALL.md` inside the zip. Code: MIT. Samples: CC0 (Karoryfer Lecolds, Versilian Studios). Font: Yellowtail (Apache 2.0).
